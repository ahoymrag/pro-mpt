# The pro-mpt Manifesto

## The Problem We're Solving

You ask AI systems hundreds of questions. Every prompt is a signal about how you think—what you're curious about, where you're stuck, what you're learning. But those signals disappear. They vanish into history once the conversation ends.

This is a tragedy for three reasons:

### 1. **You Lose Your Solutions**
Six months later, you face a similar problem. You know you solved something like this before, but you can't remember how. So you ask the AI the same question again. Or worse—you try to solve it from scratch.

**Cost**: Wasted time, repeated iterations, slower progress.

### 2. **You Can't See Your Growth**
How much smarter are you now than a year ago? You *feel* like you're learning, but you have no proof. No timeline of your evolution. No way to say "I was confused about X, now I can explain it to others."

**Cost**: Lack of confidence, impostor syndrome, inability to assess real progress.

### 3. **You Can't Prove Your Expertise**
When hiring, consulting, or collaborating, how do you prove what you actually know? A resume is guesswork. Your prompts are evidence.

Every prompt you asked and refined is a data point in your expertise. Your archive is your portfolio. But nobody sees it.

**Cost**: Missed opportunities, difficulty scaling your impact, barriers to advancement.

## Core Principles

### 1. **You Own Your Data**
Everything lives on your machine. Encrypted. Private. You export it anytime.

Not because we're being nice, but because **data ownership is non-negotiable**. Your prompts are the record of how you think. That's sacred.

### 2. **Simple, Not Complex**
One command to save: `pro-mpt log "your prompt"`
One command to find: `pro-mpt search "topic"`

No bloat. No required accounts. No API keys. Just a local database and a clean CLI.

### 3. **Learn From Reality**
We don't guess what features you need. We watch what you actually do.

Log a prompt the same way 50 times? We suggest a template.
Export monthly? We suggest auto-export.
Always filter by model? We suggest saving that as a default.

**You build the tool by using it.**

### 4. **Privacy First**
We will never:
- Sell your data
- Show ads based on your prompts
- Build a business model dependent on surveillance

When we scale, privacy is a feature, not an afterthought. End-to-end encryption, auditability, zero-knowledge proofs—whatever it takes.

### 5. **Portable, Not Trapped**
Your data is in SQLite. Standard format. Open export.

You can take your archive to any tool, share it, analyze it, or build on it. You're never locked in.

## What Makes pro-mpt Different

### vs. Bookmark/Note-Taking Tools (Notion, Obsidian)
Those tools are passive. You have to manually organize, tag, and remember what you learned.

**pro-mpt** is active. It watches what you do, spots patterns, suggests improvements, and helps you learn without friction.

### vs. Prompt Libraries (PromptBase, etc.)
Those are read-only collections of other people's prompts.

**pro-mpt** is YOUR archive. It's about your thinking, your journey, your expertise.

### vs. AI Integrations (GitHub Copilot, etc.)
Those are just smarter autocomplete.

**pro-mpt** builds your personal agent—a system that knows you deeply, learns from your patterns, and improves over time. It's not generic. It's *you*.

### vs. Big Company Solutions
Those extract your data, sell it, and lock you in.

**pro-mpt** is open, transparent, and respectful. Your data stays yours.

We believe AI is becoming infrastructure, like electricity or water.
